About VYZCORP

A community for
people who play together.

VYZCORP is an online gaming community. The idea is simple: bring players together across shared games and activities, make it easier to find a squad, and create a community people enjoy returning to.

Our approach

Community first. Games bring us together.

VYZCORP is not tied to a single play style. Members should be able to enjoy different games, improve at their own pace, and participate in ways that suit them.

◎

Respect the squad

Be welcoming, communicate clearly, and treat teammates and opponents with respect.

◇

Play fair

Value good sportsmanship, honest play, and healthy competition over toxicity.

↗

Keep growing

Share knowledge, help one another improve, and enjoy the learning curve together.

Community culture

Keep the space welcoming and focused on play.

VYZCORP is meant to be a place for games, friendship, collaboration, and shared experiences—not a home for harassment or divisive campaigning.

Respect people

No harassment, hate, targeted abuse, or intimidation. Disagreements should never become personal attacks.

Keep it game-focused

Do not use community spaces for partisan politics, campaigning, propaganda, or agenda-driven recruitment.

Compete in good faith

No cheating, malicious disruption, or deliberate sabotage of community activities.

Make room for others

Be considerate of different skill levels, schedules, backgrounds, and preferred ways to play.

Where we are today: VYZCORP is a gaming community with Dota 2 as its main game, alongside survival and co-op titles. More games can be added as members bring new interests. Event schedules and the Discord invite will be shared once they are established.
